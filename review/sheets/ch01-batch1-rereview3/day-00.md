## ch01.u3.s3-18.device.control-supply

*type* deviceSpec · *book* 3.18 p.37 (toc-unverified) · *basis* typical · *alignment* provisional

**Claim (what is being checked):** The bucket control source is a 120 V, 60 Hz supply between X1 and X2 with a small internal impedance, so its terminal voltage sags a little when a coil draws inrush current. X2 is bonded to the enclosure (grounded) in this configuration.

**Control power supply (X1/X2)** (tag SRC, source)

| Parameter | Value | Unit | Tolerance/range |
|---|---|---|---|
| volts | 120 | V | ±0 |
| zsR | 0.35 | ohm | illustrative |
| zsX | 0.35 | ohm | illustrative |

Internal impedance |Zs| about 0.5 ohm is an illustrative value; real control transformers range from tenths of an ohm to several ohms depending on VA rating. Turning the handle off opens the transformer primary, but the secondary still connects X1 to X2 through a fraction of an ohm. Before an ohms or insulation check, pull the control fuse or lift the lead.

*Reviewer notes (builder's doubts):* Typical values only; a real datasheet is needed before any value is graded.
