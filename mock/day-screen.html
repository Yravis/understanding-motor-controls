Section 1.1 · card 5 of 6

Identify every energy source

Opening a bucket door and seeing one supply is not enough. Some control enclosures also contain an interlock circuit that is fed from a different source, such as a remote panel. It often has its own warning label on the door, and some sites use orange conductors for such circuits, though that is a local convention and not a universal rule. Because its source is elsewhere, it stays live even when the bucket's own control supply is turned off. Use the drawings, the labels and the conductor colors to find every source before you decide the equipment is safe.

Field note. A label that says more than one source feeds this enclosure is an instruction to go find that other source.
